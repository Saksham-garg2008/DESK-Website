03 / ANDROID COMPANION

Connect your companion.

DESK includes a QR-code workflow for connecting with the Android Companion. Use the shortcut below to open the pairing interface.

PAIRING SHORTCUT

Ctrl + Shift + P

With DESK open, press Ctrl + Shift + P to open the QR-code generator used by the Companion connection workflow. Follow the instructions shown in DESK and the Companion app to continue pairing.

COMPANION RESOURCES

Consult the dedicated Android repository for its app details and setup instructions. The main DESK repository also contains the Companion integration.
